1 Overview

HumbleContacts ("the App", "we", "our") is a professional networking and contact management application developed by Humble Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

By using HumbleContacts, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and contact us to request deletion of any stored data.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your contacts and profile information are stored solely to provide the App's features to you.

App Preferences

We store lightweight preferences locally on your device: your chosen theme (light/dark) and notification settings. We also store your profile visibility settings (which fields are shown when you share your contact with others).

Technical Identifiers

We store a unique user ID (your Firebase UID) to associate your data. We do not collect advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or precise location data.

3 Device Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

📷 Camera

Used to scan QR codes for contact sharing and to photograph business cards for automatic contact creation via OCR. Camera access is only active when you initiate a scan or photo capture.

👤 Contacts (Read & Write)

Used to read contacts from your device address book and to save contacts back to it. This is entirely under your control — you choose which contacts to import or export.

💬 Receive SMS / Read SMS

Used only for automatic OTP detection during phone number verification. We do not read any other SMS messages and do not store SMS content.

📡 NFC

Used to share your contact card with another device via a tap. No data is transmitted to our servers over NFC.

🌐 Internet

Required for syncing data with Firebase, signing in, and using the Gemini AI business-card parser.

All runtime permissions (Camera, Contacts, SMS) are requested in-context when you first use the relevant feature. You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → HumbleContacts → Permissions.

4 How We Use Your Information

Provide App Features

Store and sync your contacts, profile, events, and reminders across sessions and devices.

Business Card Scanning (OCR + AI)

When you scan a business card, the image is processed on-device by Google ML Kit to extract text. That extracted text (not the image) is then sent to the Google Gemini API to structure the contact fields. The business card image itself may be uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage if you choose to attach it to the contact.

Authentication

Verify your identity via email/password, Google Sign-In, or phone OTP so that only you can access your data.

Contact Sharing

Generate shareable links or QR codes for your profile. Only the fields you have enabled under your share settings are visible to recipients.

Notifications & Reminders

Deliver in-app reminders for follow-ups you have scheduled.

5 Third-Party Services

We use the following Google / Firebase services. Each is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Authentication

Manages sign-in for email/password, Google, and phone OTP. Firebase stores authentication credentials securely on Google's infrastructure.

Firebase Cloud Firestore

Stores your profile, contacts, events, and reminders. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit by Google.

Firebase Cloud Storage

Stores business card images and profile photos you upload.

Google ML Kit (Text Recognition)

Runs entirely on-device to extract text from business card images. No image data is sent to Google's servers by ML Kit.

Google Gemini API

Receives the text extracted from a business card (not the image) to parse it into structured contact fields. This text may include personal information printed on the business card (name, title, phone, email). Google's data use policies for Gemini API apply.

Google Sign-In (Credential Manager)

Handles the OAuth flow for signing in with your Google account. We receive only your display name, email address, and profile photo URL.

ZXing / ZXing Android Embedded

Open-source QR code scanning library. Operates entirely on-device with no network access.

7 Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Firebase infrastructure, which provides encryption at rest (AES-256) and encryption in transit (TLS). Firebase Security Rules ensure that each user can only read and write their own data.

Business card images and profile photos are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage with access restricted by your Firebase UID.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable Google account 2-factor authentication.

8 Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you have an active account. When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your user profile document
  • All contacts you have saved
  • All events and reminders
  • All uploaded images (business cards, profile photo)
  • Your Firebase Authentication account
Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. You can export your contacts to a CSV file before deleting your account via Settings → Export Contacts.

9 Your Rights & Controls

Access

All data you have entered is visible within the App in your profile and contacts list.

Correction

You can edit your profile and any contact at any time from within the App.

Export

You can export all your contacts to a CSV file via Settings → Export Contacts. The file is saved to your device's Downloads folder.

Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete Account. This action is immediate and irreversible.

Visibility Controls

Under Profile → Share Settings you can choose exactly which fields (phone, email, company, LinkedIn) are visible when you share your contact card.

Permissions

You can revoke Camera, Contacts, or SMS permissions at any time via Android Settings. Revoking a permission disables the corresponding feature but does not affect already-stored data.
