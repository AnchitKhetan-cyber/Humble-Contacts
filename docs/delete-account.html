Delete Your Account

HumbleContacts  ·  Humble Solutions

You can permanently delete your HumbleContacts account and all associated data at any time, either directly from within the app or by submitting a request to us by email.

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. We recommend exporting your contacts first via Settings → Export Contacts.

Option 1  ·  Delete from within the App (Recommended)

This is the fastest way — deletion happens immediately.

  1. Open HumbleContacts and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (bottom navigation bar or top-right menu).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation prompt, then tap Delete to confirm.
  5. Your account and all data are deleted immediately.

Option 2  ·  Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, email us at executives@humblesolutions.in with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include:

  1. The email address or phone number linked to your HumbleContacts account.
  2. A brief statement confirming you wish to delete the account and all data.

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

Deleted Immediately & Permanently
  • Your user profile (name, email, phone, job title, company, bio, LinkedIn URL)
  • All contacts you have saved in the app
  • All events and reminders
  • All uploaded images (business card photos, profile photo)
  • Your Firebase Authentication account (sign-in credentials)
  • All app preferences and share settings
Data Retained

We do not retain any personal data after deletion. Anonymised or aggregated analytics (if any) contain no personally identifiable information and cannot be linked back to you. Firebase / Google infrastructure may hold encrypted backups for up to 180 days before they are fully purged from backup systems, in accordance with Google's data retention policy.

Contacts Exported to Your Device

If you previously exported contacts to your device's address book or a CSV file, those copies remain on your device and are not affected by account deletion. You can delete them manually from your device's Contacts app or file manager.
